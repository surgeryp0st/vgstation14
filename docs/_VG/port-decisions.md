# vgstation13 port: decisions

Decisions made so far for porting vgstation13 into vgstation14. When vg13 and SS14 both have a system, or SS14 has content vg13 doesn't, the call is made case by case and recorded here.

## Ground rules

- **Licensing:** code ported from vgstation13 or written for vgstation14 lives in `_VG` folders and is AGPLv3 (`LICENSE-AGPLv3.txt`). Upstream SS14 files stay MIT. vg13 assets are CC-BY-SA-3.0 and must credit vgstation13 in their `meta.json` / `attributions.yml`.
- **Upstream:** we keep merging space-wizards/space-station-14. vg code stays in `_VG` namespaces and folders, and edits to upstream files are kept minimal and marked `// VG` (`# VG:` in YAML).
- **Overlapping systems:** no default. Each system that exists in both vg13 and SS14 is decided on its own (see below).
- **SS14-only content** (things vg13 doesn't have): decided case by case as it comes up: keep, disable, or remove.
- **Sprites:** vg13 sprites wherever both games have the thing; SS14-only content keeps SS14 art. Convert with `Tools/_VG/dmi2rsi.py` into `Resources/Textures/_VG/`, mirroring vg13's `icons/` layout.
- **Client prediction:** everything that can be client-predicted must be, including SS14 systems we replace or rework. vg13 ran everything on the server, but ported systems put their logic in Shared so the acting player's client predicts it. Only what the engine can't predict yet (bloodstream, chat emotes, spawning) stays server-side.
- **Gameplay numbers:** default to vg13's shipped `config-example/game_options.txt`. Tunables are `vg.*` CVars.

## Plan

1. **Tooling:** dmi → rsi converter (done), dmm → SS14 map converter (started: `Tools/_VG/dmm2map/`, see Maps below).
2. **Core simulation:** health/damage and combat first, then atmos, power and the rest. Each one is decided separately.
3. **Departments:** chemistry, items/clothing, food, hydroponics, medical, research, mining, virology, genetics, robotics, cargo.
4. **Antagonists:** vg13's Dynamic mode and its factions/roles.
5. **Maps:** convert vg13 stations.
6. **Long tail:** spacepods, fission reactor, telescience, traders, and so on.

## Decisions of 2026-10-23

- **Slipping:** vg13's rules replace SS14's. Water doesn't slip people walking; lube slips walkers too, slides them 4 tiles and does 2 brute; ice is a 15% slip that slides you until you're off the ice; stun and knockdown as vg13. Cryotheum ice (below 0 C with visible cryotheum) comes with it. Done: `VgSlipSystem` (shared, predicted; `// VG` hook at the top of `SlipperySystem.TrySlip`) slips vg mobs by vg13's CheckSlip()/Slip(): on your feet and not buckled; walkers only on lube and things; galoshes and magboots (SS14's no-slip) help with all but lube. Water puddles: stun 5, knockdown 3 ticks, a 1-tile slide. Lube puddles: 5/3, 2 brute, a 5-tile slide. Ice (`VgIce`, vg13's sprite): 15% a step, 4/3, slides you off the ice. Things you step on: banana peels (and anything else slippery) 2/2, soap 3/2. `VgIceSystem` ices over floors at or below 0 C with over 0.7 mol of cryotheum and melts ice by vg13's thickness rule. Other mobs keep SS14's slipping. Puddles stand in for vg13's wet floors (their lifetimes are SS14's).
- **Fire smoke:** vg13's bad smoke from burning things (its spawn chance and density), on SS14's smoke entity, with vg13's effects. Done; it turned out vg13's choking effects never run (dead code), so the smoke blocks sight and halves beams.
- **What burns:** sweep SS14's clothing and items by vg13's categories (flammable clothing is fabric; glasses, helmets, armor, space and fire suits don't burn; other items by vg13's recycling types), with mass from item size as vg13 does. Done.
- **Drinks and fire:** only water and corn oil put fires out, as in vg13 (corn oil with vg13's rule). Firefighting foam keeps working. Done.
- **Air movement:** vg13's ZAS airflow replaces SS14 space wind's push on mobs and items, with vg13's numbers. Done: `VgAirflowSystem`, from a `// VG` hook in `AtmosphereSystem.ExperiencePressureDifference` (SS14's `atmos.space_wind` is now on by default, as vg13's airflow_push is). vg13 compares zones, so the differential is the pressure here less the lowest pressure up to 8 tiles along the flow (space counts as none). Above 60 kPa mobs are knocked down min(rand(d/20, d/10), 5) ticks (6 s cooldown; shoes save you if you couldn't slip right now). Things move by vg13's thresholds (tiny always, small 20, normal 35, larger 50, mobs 65 unless buckled, grabbed or in active magboots, other loose objects 85; 3 s apart) at vg13's airflow speed toward the low side; a person slamming into something takes speed x 2 brute over head, chest and groin (melee armor counts), a knockdown and a stun.
- **Body temperature:** vg13's model for vg mobs (environment exchange, clothing heat and cold protection, species heat and cold levels and damage, hypothermia stages); SS14's TemperatureSystem skips them.
- **Pulling (2026-10-24):** vg13's step pull for vg mobs: what you pull moves into the tile you just left, one tile behind at any speed, instead of SS14's physics joint. Done: `VgPullController` (shared physics controller) records the puller's path and moves the pulled thing to the point a tile back along it once it's no longer adjacent, so it follows round corners; SS14's pull joint isn't made for vg pullers (`// VG` in `PullingSystem.TryStartPull`), and the pull breaks past 3 tiles. Grabs ride on it unchanged.
- **Hallucinations:** all of vg13's, client-side for the hallucinating player. Done (see Status effects).

## Atmospherics

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Decided: **keep SS14's atmos engine** (tile-based LINDA/Monstermos, predicted-friendly, upstream-maintained) and port vg13's gases and rules onto it, not ZAS (zones sharing one mixture). Mob code already reads the engine's API. | Done |
| Gases | vg13's gases: oxygen, nitrogen, CO2, plasma, N2O (both games) plus volatile fuel, oxygen agent B, cryotheum and radon, added to the Gas enum (13 gases) with vg13's specific heats and molar masses (nitrogen heat 20, plasma 405 g/mol). SS14-only gases (tritium, water vapor, ammonia, frezon) are **disabled**: reactions commented out, canisters, miners and crate contents removed, maps migrated; prototypes kept. Overlays are vg13's (`_VG/effects/tile_effects.rsi`): plasma and cryotheum show above 0.7 mol in a tile and N2O above 1, all at once (SS14's fade is cut to almost nothing); fuel, agent B and radon are invisible. vg13 picks one of three (cryotheum: six) variants per tile at random; only the first is used, since SS14's overlay has one state per gas. | Done |
| Fire | Decided: the **full vg13 fire model**, on SS14's atmos. `VgFireSystem` replaces SS14's hotspots (a `// VG` hook in `AtmosphereSystem.HotspotExpose` hands every ignition to it; SS14's PlasmaFire and TritiumFire reactions are off). A burning tile has a `VgFire` entity that burns plasma and volatile fuel (vg13's zburn: firelevel, 25 fire-level multiplier, fuel burning 5x as fast), burns everything flammable on it, burns mobs on it (SS14 fire stacks plus vg13's burn damage), heats the air and spreads. Flammable things are `VgBurnable` (vg13 flammable + thermal_material + thermal_mass; materials wood, plastic, fabric, wax, biological with vg13's numbers): they autoignite in hot, oxygenated air or when a fire touches them, burn their mass away, give heat, oxygen use and CO2 to the fire, and ash. Paper burns so far. Liquid fuels burn too (plasma, welding fuel and ethanol in containers or puddles, vg13's numbers; glycerol waits for the vg13 reagents), and the flames and light grow with the fire level and take vg13's heat2color() of the air temperature, refreshed every 10 +/- 2 s as in vg13. **Putting fires out:** water is vg13's (`VgDouseTileReaction` on `Water`: the tile's fire goes, everything on the tile is put out and can't catch for 30 s, and the air cools 0.1-0.5 K but not below 20 C), so extinguishers, buckets and spills work. Only water and corn oil put fires out, as in vg13 (decided 2026-10-23): SS14's drinks lost their extinguishing; water keeps putting out burning people on touch; corn oil (`VgOilQuenchTileReaction`) puts the fire out and drops the air to min(T - 2000, T / 2). SS14's `ExtinguishTileReaction` (holy water) sees vg13 fires through `// VG` hooks in `AtmosphereSystem.IsHotspotActive`/`HotspotExtinguish`. **Floors:** vg13's flammable floors (wood, grass, carpet, arcade; `vgFlammableTile` prototypes map SS14 tiles to vg13 materials, 5 thermal mass) burn: the fire system spawns a hidden `VgFloorFuel` burnable on the tile when a fire or hot air first reaches it, which burns like any burnable and stays, spent, once burnt (vg13 turfs don't ash); replacing the tile resets it. SS14's carpet entities burn the same way. **Plasma walls** (`VgPlasmaBurn` on SS14's `WallPlasma`): vg13's PlasmaBurn(): a hot item (a lit welder 3480 K, other flames 700 K) or a beam (any SS14 hitscan, 2500 K) sets one off above 300 K; it leaves its girder, puts 20 mol of plasma at 400 C on its tile with a hotspot, and sets off plasma walls within 3 tiles at a quarter of the temperature. So a welder takes a plasma wall up rather than apart, as in vg13. Not ported: ion bolts (500 K; SS14's are projectiles), plasma airlocks and false walls (SS14 has none). **Gas in containers** (`VgContainedBurn` reaction): vg13 react() burns plasma or volatile fuel with oxygen in pipes, tanks and canisters past 100 C by itself (zburn() with no tile); gas on floors still only burns where a fire is. **Smoke** (decided 2026-10-23: SS14's smoke entity, vg13's effects): burning things give off `VgFireSmoke` with vg13's genSmoke() odds (4% a burn at 20 C, none at 1000 C) and puffs (ceil(5 * (0.14 / oxygen ratio)^2), 1 to 5), lasting 10 s, on vg13's 96x96 smoke sprite. It blocks sight, and a hitscan beam loses half its damage for each puff it passes through (`// VG` hook in the server `GunSystem`). vg13's bad-smoke choking (drop items, oxygen damage, cough) is dead code there (its base affect() returns early for carbon mobs), so it isn't ported. Not yet: nothing else listed. **What burns** (decided 2026-10-23: sweep by vg13's categories): `vgFlammable` prototypes, generated by `Tools/_VG/flammable/gen_flammable.py`, say for every SS14 entity whether it burns, with vg13's material and mass (from item size, vg13's w_class table; vg13's own masses for storage, trash and furniture); `VgFlammableSystem` gives matching entities a `VgBurnable` as they are created, and new prototypes inherit their nearest listed parent's entry.
