# vgstation13 port: decisions

Decisions made so far for porting vgstation13 into vgstation14. When vg13 and SS14 both have a system, or SS14 has content vg13 doesn't, the call is made case by case and recorded here.

## Ground rules

- **Licensing:** code ported from vgstation13 or written for vgstation14 lives in `_VG` folders and is AGPLv3 (`LICENSE-AGPLv3.txt`). Upstream SS14 files stay MIT. vg13 assets are CC-BY-SA-3.0 and must credit vgstation13 in their `meta.json` / `attributions.yml`.
- **Upstream:** we keep merging space-wizards/space-station-14. vg code stays in `_VG` namespaces and folders, and edits to upstream files are kept minimal and marked `// VG` (`# VG:` in YAML).
- **Overlapping systems:** no default. Each system that exists in both vg13 and SS14 is decided on its own (see below).
- **SS14-only content** (things vg13 doesn't have): decided case by case as it comes up: keep, disable, or remove.
- **Sprites:** vg13 sprites wherever both games have the thing; SS14-only content keeps SS14 art. Convert with `Tools/_VG/dmi2rsi.py` into `Resources/Textures/_VG/`, mirroring vg13's `icons/` layout.
- **Client prediction:** everything that can be client-predicted must be, including SS14 systems we replace or rework. vg13 ran everything on the server, but ported systems put their logic in Shared so the acting player's client predicts it. Only what the engine can't predict yet (bloodstream, chat emotes, spawning) stays server-side.
- **Gameplay numbers:** default to vg13's shipped `config-example/game_options.txt`. Tunables are `vg.*` CVars.

## Plan

1. **Tooling:** dmi → rsi converter (done), dmm → SS14 map converter (next).
2. **Core simulation:** health/damage and combat first, then atmos, power and the rest. Each one is decided separately.
3. **Departments:** chemistry, items/clothing, food, hydroponics, medical, research, mining, virology, genetics, robotics, cargo.
4. **Antagonists:** vg13's Dynamic mode and its factions/roles.
5. **Maps:** convert vg13 stations.
6. **Long tail:** spacepods, fission reactor, telescience, traders, and so on.

## Health, damage and combat

Decided: the **full vg13 model**.

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Damage types | vg13's Brute, Burn, Toxin, Oxygen, Clone. Brain damage lives on the brain organ and halloss in the pain system, not as damage types, as in vg13. | Done |
| Upstream damage content | SS14 type names stay valid in YAML/C# and are translated when prototypes load: Blunt→Brute; Slash→Brute with sharpness 1 + edge; Piercing→Brute with sharpness 1; Heat/Cold/Shock/Caustic→Burn; Poison/Radiation→Toxin; Asphyxiation/Bloodloss→Oxygen; Cellular→Clone. Structural and Holy are unchanged. | Done |
| Limbs | Per-limb damage on vg13's 11 limbs (including groin): wounds, fractures, internal bleeding, dismemberment, infection, robotic and peg limbs. Only organic, attached limbs count towards health. Fractures and dismemberment are on, as in vg13's config. | In progress |
| Body system | **Replace** SS14's body/organ system with vg13's. Order: vg13 internal organs → blood → breathing → chemistry and metabolism → remove SS14's body (hands and legs from vg13 limbs) and convert species. | In progress: internal organs done (heart, lungs, liver, kidneys, brain, appendix, eyes, with vg13 damage from limb hits, kidney filtering, liver toxin damage, bruised lungs, blind broken eyes, brain damage effects); blood done (vg13 regeneration, heart efficiency and blood loss effects on SS14's blood solution, which stays the vessel; SS14's own regeneration, bleeding and bloodloss damage no longer run on vg13 bodies) |
| Metabolism, breathing, digestion | Replace SS14's organ-based versions with vg13's: mob-level reagent metabolism, breathing in the Life() tick, food reagents going straight into the body. | Breathing done: vg13 breath (internals or the tile, 0.5 l), lung gas rules (O2 16-140 kPa, CO2 overload, plasma toxicity with gas-mask relief, N2O), breath temperature burns, losebreath; SS14's respirator no longer runs on vg13 bodies. Metabolism and digestion come with chemistry. |
| Controls | vg13's four intents (help/disarm/grab/harm) and zone selector, replacing SS14 combat mode. Pure vg13: SS14's wide swing (heavy attack) and combat-mode shove are removed; harm is a single click attack, disarm uses vg13's odds. Items no interaction handles hit mobs on any intent and other things on harm intent. Guns fire at anything out of reach on any intent, point blank on adjacent mobs only on harm. Grab intent is an SS14 pull until grab levels are ported; stuns are SS14 knockdowns until the stun model is. | Done (predicted) |
| Zone selector | vg13's body-doll sprite, click regions and hotkeys, placed beside SS14's hotbar/hands so it fits the SS14 HUD layout (not vg13's exact corner). Midnight style (vg13's default). Hotkeys are vg13's: Ctrl+1-4, Insert, Ctrl/Alt+Numpad. | Done |
| Stuns | vg13's model: no stamina; stunned/weakened/paralysis; vg13 disarm odds (40% miss, 40% knockdown). Stun weapons both games have (stun baton, taser) use vg13's numbers: one-hit stuns, ~20 s. Pain and traumatic shock (halloss, pain_shock_stage) come with it. Stuns map onto SS14's status effects with vg13 durations (Life() ticks × 2 s). Not yet: eye blur and jitter (wait on vg13 eyes), painkillers (wait on chemistry). | Done (predicted) |
| SS14-only stamina damage | Disablers, beanbag/rubber/baton-grenade rounds, cane, cowbar, boxing gloves, toy hammer, luminous person, tesla gun, anti-materiel round: their stamina damage becomes vg13 halloss, feeding pain shock. | Done |
| Grabs | vg13's grab levels: passive → aggressive → neck → strangle. A grab rides on an SS14 pull (dragging uses SS14's pull physics), with vg13's grab item in hand: use it or click the victim to tighten, drop it to let go, throw it (aggressive+) to throw them. Struggling free is vg13's 25% (aggressive) / 5% (neck) per try. One grab at a time for now; losebreath is approximated until vg13 breathing is in. | Done (predicted; the grab item spawns server-side) |
| Armor | vg13's armor: melee/bullet/laser/energy/bomb/bio/rad categories, per-limb coverage, additive percentages, armor penetration. SS14-only clothing has its coefficients translated when loaded. Clothing both games have gets vg13's armor values in the armor port. Done through a `vgClothing` table keyed by entity id (upstream clothing YAML untouched; `Tools/_VG/clothing/` regenerates it). Melee and punches check melee armor at the aimed limb; projectiles and hitscan hit the shooter's zone with vg13's miss chance and check bullet or laser armor; explosions are scaled by average bomb armor (until explosions themselves are ported). SS14 armor and worn explosion resistance no longer apply to vg13 mobs. | Done |
| Examine | vg13's examine text for limbs: wounds by stage (bleeding/bandaged/infected), missing limbs, peg and robot limbs, splints, open incisions. Uniform, gloves, shoes and gas masks hide what's under them; bleeding still shows through. Predicted. | Done |
| Wound treatment items | Replace SS14's bruise packs, ointment and gauze with vg13's medical stacks (bruise pack, ointment, advanced trauma/burn kits, splints) and their behaviour: bandage/salve/splint the aimed limb's wounds. SS14's `Brutepack`/`Gauze`/`Ointment` ids are kept as hidden aliases of vg13's roll of gauze and ointment, so maps, fills and recipes give vg13 items. SS14's advanced medkit holds vg13's advanced first-aid kit contents. Predicted. | Done |
| SS14-only medical items | Tourniquet, blood pack, regenerative mesh, medicated suture, aloe cream, healing toolbox: **disabled for now**. Taken out of vendors, lockers and maps, prototypes kept so this can be revisited once vg13 medical is in. References are `# VG:` comments; `migration.yml` drops them from maps; their microwave recipes are commented out. | Done |
| Natural healing | SS14's passive regeneration is removed for organic species; healing happens through vg13 wounds. | Done |

## Chemistry

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Chemistry | **Replace** SS14's reagents and recipes with vg13's, like the body system. Infection waits for it (spaceacillin). | In progress: metabolism framework and first medicines (below) |
| Chemistry engine | Keep SS14's solution/reaction engine (containers, machines, transfers, prediction); replace its data: reagents, recipes, effects and metabolism become vg13's. vg13-only engine mechanics (container-specific reactions, slime extracts, ...) are added as `_VG` extensions. | In progress: vg13 reagents are ordinary `reagent` prototypes with vg13's lowercase ids (no clash with SS14's CamelCase ids), plus a `vgReagent` prototype of the same id holding `on_mob_life` as data (metabolism rate, overdose, pain resistance, a list of effects). `VgMetabolismSystem` (server, since the bloodstream is) runs them on the Life() tick over the bloodstream's chemical solution, with vg13's tick counters, overdose and liver-rate removal. Effects so far: heal limb damage, adjust tox/oxy/clone, brain loss, losebreath, purge other reagents. Ported with their vg13 recipes: dylovene, bicaridine, kelotane, dexalin, inaprovaline, tricordrazine (inputs are still SS14's elements until the basic reagents are replaced). Also: synthocarisol, rezadone (tick-ranged effects via `minTick`/`maxTick`; face repair and recipe wait), alkysine, alkycosine, dermaline, dexalin plus, cryoxadone, clonexadone (heal only when the body is cold, via a temperature event answered server-side), and the painkillers paracetamol, oxycodone and tramadol. Painkillers work as in vg13: reagent pain resistance is summed onto `VgStatusComponent` each tick and subtracted from the pain level; oxycodone numbs pain (`pain_numb`) and tramadol speeds shock recovery. Effects have to live in Shared, since the client loads the prototypes too. Also: activated charcoal (purges the body's other reagents, 5% vomit), lexorin, imidazoline (heals eyes), peridaxon (heals chest organs), leporazine (pulls body temperature to 310 K), synaptizine (drowsiness, pain resistance; its stun reduction, hallucination and mindbreaker removal wait on those systems). Next: the rest of vg13's medicines, then the dispensers. |
| Basic reagents | Decided: replace with vg13's. The element reagents keep SS14's ids (both games have the same list), and vg13's mob behaviour is attached through `vgReagent` entries with the same id, so no recipe or jug needs renaming. Done: chlorine (brute), fluorine (toxin), lithium overdose, radium (radiation dose). Waiting on other systems: lithium drifting. | In progress |
| Toxins | First batch of vg13's toxins: toxin, carpotoxin, amatoxin, mercury (SS14 ids, vg13 behaviour) and new cyanide, chloramine, potassium hydroxide. The rest (amanitin, mindbreaker, mutagens, sleep toxin, ...) come with the systems they need (vomiting, drugs, sleep, genetics). | In progress |
| Nutrition | Decided: **remove thirst** and use vg13's hunger exactly. `VgNutritionComponent` (400 start, burns 0.15 per Life() tick, 25% less asleep, overeat counter) replaces SS14's Hunger and Thirst on vg mobs. Food and drink reagents feed through a `nutrimentFactor` on their `vgReagent` (vg13 numbers; SS14's Protein/Fat/Vitamin feed like nutriment). Eating and drinking put the reagents straight into the bloodstream (`VgIngestionSystem`, with `// VG` hooks in `FoodSystem` and `DrinkSystem`); the Life() tick metabolises them. Hungry/starving alerts and examine text as vg13; below 400 nutrition slows you like vg13's movement tally. Starvation damage only exists in vg13's hardcore mode (off by default), so it is not ported. Overeating makes you fat as vg13 does (500 ticks over 450 nutrition, fit again under 100, 1.5x slower). Still to do: fat sprites and clothing fit, the remaining reagents vg13 names differently, vomit's nutrition cost. | In progress |
| Status effects | Jitter, druggy (seeing rainbows) and drowsiness are vg13 Life()-tick statuses (`VgStatusSystem`), onto SS14's jitter and seeing-rainbows effects. Reagent effects can now be tick-ranged and chance-based (`minTick`/`maxTick`/`chance`). Ported with them: space drugs, impedrezene (SS14 ids, vg13 behaviour and recipes), caffeine. Vomiting is vg13's (`VgVomitSystem`, raised with `VgVomitEvent`/the `VgVomit` reagent effect): warning, second warning 15 s later, stun and throw up 10 s after that for -40 nutrition and +3 toxin healed, 35 s before the next. Still to do: dizziness, confusion, hallucination, slurring. | In progress |
| Radiation | Decided: vg13's radiation dose. `VgRadiationComponent` (dose + rad_tick) takes over from SS14's instant radiation damage on vg mobs (a `// VG` early-out in `DamageableSystem.OnIrradiated`); sources add to the dose, cut by the mob's radiation armor. Life() tick: vg13's dose ladder (toxin damage, collapse), then long-term organ and clone damage. Radium adds to the dose; hyronalin and arithrazine (SS14 ids, vg13 behaviour and recipes) treat it. Radiation sickness now makes you vomit. Still to do: nosebleeds, hallucination, internal bleeding, limb mutation, blindness and necrosis from long exposure, and arithrazine's symptom block. | In progress |
| Chem dispenser | Decided: replace with vg13's. SS14's jug-based machine and UI stay; the stock list becomes vg13's (iodine out, water and sulfuric acid in; tungsten waits for its reagent). Ported medicines join once the basic elements are vg13's. | In progress |
| SS14-only reagents | Food, drink and juice reagents **stay**, rewritten for vg13 metabolism. SS14-only medicines, toxins, narcotics and fun/misc chems are **disabled** (out of dispensers, recipes, vendors and starting contents; items needing one get the closest vg13 reagent; prototypes kept). Species bloods follow their species; gases (frezon, tritium, N2O) wait for atmos. | Planned |

## Atmospherics

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Decided: **keep SS14's atmos engine** (tile-based LINDA/Monstermos, predicted-friendly, upstream-maintained) and port vg13's gases and rules onto it, not ZAS (zones sharing one mixture). Mob code already reads the engine's API. | Done |
| Gases | vg13's gases: oxygen, nitrogen, CO2, plasma, N2O (both games) plus volatile fuel, oxygen agent B, cryotheum and radon, added to the Gas enum (13 gases) with vg13's specific heats and molar masses (nitrogen heat 20, plasma 405 g/mol). SS14-only gases (tritium, water vapor, ammonia, frezon) are **disabled**: reactions commented out, canisters, miners and crate contents removed, maps migrated; prototypes kept. | Done (overlay sprites missing) |
| Fire | Decided: the **full vg13 fire model**, on SS14's atmos. `VgFireSystem` replaces SS14's hotspots (a `// VG` hook in `AtmosphereSystem.HotspotExpose` hands every ignition to it; SS14's PlasmaFire and TritiumFire reactions are off). A burning tile has a `VgFire` entity that burns plasma and volatile fuel (vg13's zburn: firelevel, 25 fire-level multiplier, fuel burning 5x as fast), burns everything flammable on it, burns mobs on it (SS14 fire stacks plus vg13's burn damage), heats the air and spreads. Flammable things are `VgBurnable` (vg13 flammable + thermal_material + thermal_mass; materials wood, plastic, fabric, wax, biological with vg13's numbers): they autoignite in hot, oxygenated air or when a fire touches them, burn their mass away, give heat, oxygen use and CO2 to the fire, and ash. Paper burns so far. Liquid fuels burn too (plasma, welding fuel and ethanol in containers or puddles, vg13's numbers; glycerol waits for the vg13 reagents), and the flames and light grow with the fire level. Not yet: burning floors and walls, smoke, heat colour, extinguishers and foam, canisters/pipes burning gas (zburn from tanks), and marking vg13 items flammable as they are ported. | In progress |
| Reactions | vg13's N2O decomposition (above 300 C, pressure-scaled), cryotheum with oxygen and cryotheum with plasma. Radon irradiates mobs standing in it (1.5 per standard-tile mole, every Life() tick). Not yet: vg13's fire (flammable objects, firelevel), plasma contamination, volatile fuel and oxygen agent B behaviour, canisters/miners for the new gases, vg13's pressure and airflow numbers. | In progress |

## Power

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | Decided: **keep SS14's power engine** (cables, APCs, SMES, batteries, solars) and make it behave like vg13 on top; retune numbers to vg13's; SS14-only generators and the HV/MV/LV tiers follow the keep/disable rule when reached. | Decided |
| Area channels | Decided: vg13's area-based APC channels. SS14 has no areas, so an APC's network is its area: every machine linked to the APC is on its equipment, lighting or environment channel (lights are lighting; doors, vents, scrubbers and air alarms are environment; the rest equipment; `VgPowerChannel` overrides). A channel that is off gives its machines no power and zero load (a `// VG` term in `PowerNetSystem.UpdateApcPowerReceiver`). `VgApcChannelSystem` runs vg13's channel states (off, auto-off, on, auto-on): below 30% battery with no charging in sight equipment goes, below 15% lighting too, an empty battery takes everything, and `longtermpower` stops a short dip from cutting anything. The APC window has off/on/auto buttons per channel. | Done |
| Numbers | APC: vg13's 2500-unit cell (1.25 MJ, 90% at start, charging capped at 1.25 kW); SMES: 5 MJ starting at 1 MJ, 200 kW output and 50 kW charging (vg13's defaults; SS14 has no setting UI to change them). Solar output, cells, machine draws: next. | In progress |
| Next | power alerts, vg13-only power machines. | Planned |

## Species and movement

| Area | Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Shared species | Become vg13's versions (body layout, organs, flags, stats, sprites): Human, Diona, Vox; SS14 Reptilian becomes Unathi, Slime person becomes vg13 Slime, Skeleton becomes Skellington. | In progress: `vgSpecies` prototypes hold vg13's species numbers (`VgSpeciesComponent` on the mob): unarmed damage range, punch damage, claws and sharpness, attack verb, move speed mod/multiplier, which internal organs the body has, and the temperature levels (stored for the temperature port). Human, Unathi (the Reptilian, renamed) and Vox (nitrogen breathing, oxygen poisoning through breath and through the reagent, cold levels) are done; reagent effects can be limited to species with `onlySpecies`/`exceptSpecies`; punches, movement and the body read them. Skellington, Diona and Slime too: species flags (`noBreathe`, `noBlood`, `noPain`, `radAbsorb`) and organ lists apply at map init, Diona absorb radiation as nutrition and healing, and brute, burn, toxin and radiation mods (Skellington takes double brute, Diona 2.5x burn, Slime double toxin) are applied as in vg13. The skeleton gets the vg body pieces directly since it isn't an organic mob. Sprites: the six shared species' body parts are vg13's (`Tools/_VG/species_rsi.py` slices vg13's per-limb species .dmi into SS14's part states, draws the groin onto the torso, paints over the eye placeholders, and divides out the skin tint SS14 multiplies by for Humans so the default skin looks like vg13's; the other species keep vg13's own colours and use SS14's near-white tinted-hues skin colouring, except Slime which keeps Hues since vg13 slimes are tinted too); hair, markings, eyes and skin-tone selection stay SS14's. Next: Diona's light/photosynthesis, special organs (Diona liver, Vox variants, slime core), sprites, and the vg13-only species. |
| vg13-only species | Add the player-selectable ones: Tajaran, Skrell, Grey, Insectoid, Mushroom, Plasmaman. Special ones (Golem, Muton, Ghoul, vampire forms) come with the antags/events that make them. | In progress: all six are selectable, with vg13's numbers (damage mods, claws, breath, organs, temperature levels) and vg13 body sprites. Missing: Tajaran ears and tail, Plasmaman's ignition and containment suits, Grey water vulnerability and telepathy, Mushroom's missing mouth, species-specific organs, names and clothing sprites. |
| SS14-only species | Arachnid, Moth, Dwarf, Gingerbread: **disabled for now** (not selectable, prototypes kept, existing characters fall back to Human). | Planned |
| Movement speed | vg13's movement delays: base run/walk speeds and all slowdowns (shock, missing/broken legs, hunger, ...) as in vg13. vg13's delay (tally × multipliers, quantized to its 0.33 ds tick) becomes SS14 speeds: running 7.6 tiles/s, walking 2.3. In: grabs, damage, shock, legs (canes help), bare feet, clothing slowdowns (from the `vgClothing` table, replacing SS14 clothing speed modifiers and SlowOnDamage). Still to come with their systems: hunger, cold, drowsiness, chemicals, floors. | Done (partly) |
